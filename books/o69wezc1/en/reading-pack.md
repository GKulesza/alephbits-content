## Text

**THE GREEN GLOW**

## I. THE BEGINNING

Krzysztof sat before the microphone in his study, looking at the green glow that seeped through the gap in the curtains. Outside the window Warsaw was drowning in night lights, but he saw something else — he saw boundaries blurring, and a future coming like a storm.

– Good evening, everyone – he said, switching on the microphone. – Welcome to the Green Glow, our entertainment series, in which, for purely entertainment purposes, we summon various conspiracy theories from the depths of the internet.

He smiled to himself. "For entertainment purposes" – that was his mantra, his shield. He talked about theories, but in reality he talked about truths that didn't yet have a name. And he knew that today would be different.

– Today it's a question-and-answer format – he continued. – It's you who decide what we talk about.

He looked at the screen, where the first questions were appearing. His gaze stopped on one:

_"What do the theories say about the military already possessing the yellow powder from Alois Irlmaier's vision? Could the fulfilment of his vision be near?"_

– Now, that's a very good question – he said, his voice becoming more focused. – Starting with the second part... the fulfilment of his vision seems to be exceptionally near. Because of the geopolitical situation.

He closed his eyes. And then it came – not as words, not as images. As _knowledge_. Someone was telling him something he wasn't supposed to know. Someone who was called "one of them".

– Indeed, the situation in which Germany has become the biggest ally of an ungrateful land... the probability that Russia will decide merely to march through the territory of Poland and fight Germany is plausible. Just as it's plausible that China will strike Russia.

He opened his eyes and looked into the camera.

– It was all in that prophecy. You can clearly see that Russia is coming to terms with the Americans. And if so, then against the Chinese. And then the Chinese may want to make a pre-emptive strike.

He paused. He knew that what he was about to say would be controversial. But he couldn't stop.

– The yellow powder – he said slowly. – It was supposed to be used to separate Western Europe from Eastern and Central Europe. It was supposed to be thrown over a very narrow strip from the north to the south of Europe. It had such properties that whatever was touched by that powder would die. Whatever crossed through that curtain would die too.

He saw the comments on the screen. People were asking whether it was possible. Whether it already existed.

– There is no information – he answered. – Even in the deepest depths of the dark web, that such a yellow powder already exists. But that doesn't mean it won't be created. That doesn't mean it isn't already in the preparation phase.

---
## II. THE DEVELOPMENT

After the broadcast Krzysztof couldn't sleep. He lay in bed, staring at the ceiling, and in his head resounded words that were not his own. Someone spoke to him all night. Someone who was called "one of them".

The next morning he got up earlier than usual. He poured himself some coffee and sat down at the computer. He opened the file where he kept his "feelings" – what came to him in moments of silence. He scrolled to an entry from three years ago:

_"One of them talked about preparations that prolong youth. He talked about reversing ageing. He talked about 20 years. Exactly 20. Not 19, not 21. 20."_

He rubbed his eyes. Back then he had laughed at it. Now he was reading news about a treatment that reverses biological age by 20 years. About tests on animals that had passed successfully. About tests on humans that were giving "wow effects".

– How did he know? – he whispered to himself. – How did he know it would reverse it by 20 years?

He got up and walked to the window. Outside, Warsaw was waking to life, but he saw something else – he saw a future in which people lived 200, 300, 400 years. He saw a population growing at an alarming rate. And he saw those who ruled the world saying that there are too many people.

– It doesn't add up – he told himself. – On one hand they say the population is too large and needs to be reduced. On the other hand they announce a preparation for eternal youth.

He sat back down at the computer. He knew he had to understand this. That he had to answer the question that had tormented him for years.

Who is "one of them"?

---
## III. THE TURNING POINT

Three days later Krzysztof received a letter. It was addressed by hand, with no sender, but he knew who it came from. He opened it with trembling hands.

_"Mr Krzysztof"_ – he read. _"I heard your broadcast about the yellow powder. About the preparations that prolong youth. About the Nightingale prophecy. I want to tell you something you shouldn't know. But you already know it, don't you? You always know."_

He felt cold. He read on:

_"The preparation exists. It works. It reverses ageing by 20 years. But there is one condition. Not money. Not subordination. Something else. Something they don't talk about in the media. Please listen to this broadcast to the end. Please understand that what they call an 'elixir of youth' is only the beginning. Then the Nightingale prophecy will come. Degradation. Suffering. And there will be no way to die."_

Krzysztof put down the letter and looked out of the window. The green glow was still seeping through the gap in the curtains. He knew it wasn't a coincidence. That someone had been leading him all along.

He sat down before the microphone. He switched it on.

– Good evening, everyone – he said. – Welcome to the Green Glow.

He fell silent for a moment. And then he did what he always did – he told the truth.

– Today I want to talk about something they call the "elixir of youth". About the preparation that reverses ageing by 20 years. About tests on animals that passed successfully. About tests on humans that give wow effects.

He paused. He knew the next sentence would be the hardest.

– But I also want to talk about the Nightingale prophecy. About the fact that people will take the preparation, be satisfied for a while, and then it will turn out that their organisms begin to degrade. And that they won't be able to die.

He saw the comments on the screen. People were asking whether it was true. Whether there was evidence.

– There are theories – he answered. – Theories that say the preparation itself will be bound up with deep immorality. That either its production will be bound up with something highly immoral... or that the right to take the preparation will be hedged around with something immoral.

He looked into the camera. In his eyes appeared the shadow of something he couldn't name.

– Imagine – he said quietly. – Imagine that in order to make the preparation lengthen your telomeres... you have to obtain, for example, a certain substance that is produced in the human body between the third and fourth month of foetal development. And in order to make these preparations, human organisms are grown in artificial wombs and that substance is extracted, which kills those organisms.

He fell silent. He knew that what he had said was shocking. But he also knew he had to say it.

– It's just a theory – he added quickly. – Just a theory. But if it's even partly true... it means that the price of creating these preparations is the mass slaughter of unborn children.

He heard his own voice, but as if from a distance. As if someone else were speaking through him.

– And then everything changes.

---
## IV. THE ENDING

A week passed. Krzysztof received a second letter. This time it was shorter:

_"You've said too much. Please stop. For your own safety."_

He didn't listen.

In the next broadcast he talked about a false-flag operation, about plans to drag Poland into a war, about how Ukrainians or Germans could attack Poland with Russian drones and convince us it was Russia. He talked about the NATO summit in Turkey, about President Nawrocki, about a meeting with Zelensky. He talked about what he knew, even though no one wanted to hear it.

And then, in the last broadcast before the break, he said something that changed everything:

– Do you remember what one of them said about Jesus? – he asked. – He said he respects him. We wondered whether he was telling the truth. Whether he was lying. But maybe... maybe it's neither truth nor lie. Maybe it's something third. Maybe it's a warning.

He fell silent for a moment.

– Maybe one of them is telling us that Jesus is worthy of respect, because he wants us to follow him. Before it's too late. Before the time comes when people will regret having taken the preparation. Before the Nightingale prophecy comes.

He looked into the camera. Tears appeared in his eyes.

– I don't know who one of them is. I don't know where he knows what he knows. But I know one thing: if he's right... if what he says is true... then we have to wake up. We have to understand that what they call progress may be a trap. That what they call the elixir of youth may be the beginning of the end.

He switched off the microphone and sat in the darkness for a long time. Outside the window the green glow was still seeping through the gap in the curtains.

He knew this wasn't the end. That it was only the beginning.

And that he had to tell the truth. Even if no one wanted to listen. Even if it cost him his life.

Because he knew it was the only thing he could do.

And that it would have to be enough.

---
## V. EPILOGUE

A month later Krzysztof disappeared. His last broadcast was removed from the internet. His flat was empty. Neighbours said they had seen him packing suitcases and leaving in the middle of the night.

No one knew where he had gone.

But in the dark corners of the internet, in places where no one looks, a new file appeared. A recording. Krzysztof's voice, quieter than usual, more tired:

_"If you're hearing this... it means I'm still alive. And that I have something to tell you. Something I didn't say in any broadcast. About the yellow powder. About the preparation. About one of them. About the green glow."_

He paused. And then he said something that changed everything:

_"One of them is not human. It's something that came from outside. Something that knows the future, because it creates it. Something that wants to warn us... but can't speak directly. It can only hint. Through people like me."_

Another pause.

_"The yellow powder exists. Not in yellow. In a colour you haven't seen yet. And it will be used. Not this year. Not next year. But it will."_

The voice became barely audible.

_"The preparation exists. It works. It reverses ageing by 20 years. But it's only bait. The real goal is population control. Those who take it will live... but they won't be able to die. And they will regret it every day."_

At the end they heard something that sounded like crying:

_"The green glow. It's not a colour. It's a signal. Someone is watching us. And someone wants us to know."_

The recording broke off.

No one ever saw Krzysztof again. But on the internet, in the dark corners, new recordings kept appearing. New warnings. New fragments of a truth no one wanted to hear.

And every time someone played them, they saw the same thing: a green glow.

---
**THE END**
