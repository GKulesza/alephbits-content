**WIND OVER THE RIVER**

Anna stood on the boulevard and felt the cool wind over the Vistula. The city hummed in the background, but here, by the water, everything seemed slower.

An older man in a navy jacket sat down beside her. For a moment they were silent. Then he said:

— Today the wind remembers everything the water carried.

Anna smiled.

— And how do you know that?

— Because I come back here every day — he answered. — And each time the wind brings something different.

Anna looked at the river. The waves reflected the lantern light in short, quiet flashes. She thought that maybe this is what a moment looks like when it is worth stopping the rush.

The man stood up, nodded, and walked toward the bridge. Anna stayed a few more minutes, listening to the wind. When she finally started back, she already knew she would return tomorrow.

**THE END**
