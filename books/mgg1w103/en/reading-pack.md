## Text

**CANDY FROM THE MAGIC TREE**

Deep in a great forest, beyond seven rivers, lived three friends — Kicek the rabbit, Kolczuś the hedgehog, and Rudy the fox. Every day they met in their treehouse to play, sing, and share adventures.

Kicek was the fastest and cheeriest of them all. He loved to run and always won at tag. Kolczuś, despite his many spines, was the gentlest animal in the forest. He knew how to listen and give advice, and his favourite game was riddles. Rudy the fox was clever and knew every corner of the forest, but sometimes he forgot that others had needs too.

One day, while playing pirates in a forest clearing, something extraordinary happened. Kicek, hiding behind a bush, got a thorn stuck in his paw. Kolczuś immediately helped him and gently pulled it out. And then — to everyone's astonishment — the tree under which they were resting began to move its branches, and one of the leaves turned into a colourful, shiny candy.

— Wow! — cried Kicek.

When Kolczuś touched the candy, it came off without any trouble. He shared it with his friends. Rudy was disappointed — he too wanted to get a candy, but he could not pick it.

— Why could you and I couldn't? — he asked.

— Maybe because I helped Kicek selflessly — answered the hedgehog. — I didn't expect anything in return.

Rudy decided that the next day he would help everyone to get a whole pile of candies. But his help was not selfless — each time he expected a reward. He helped a raccoon pump up a bicycle tyre, but only because he wanted a ride. He helped Mrs. Squirrel carry her shopping, but in return he took an apple and a banana.

And each time he ran to the magic tree, there was no candy there.

— It's not fair! — Rudy fumed. — I helped!

In the end, his friends explained that the tree only rewards those who help from the heart, expecting nothing in return. Rudy understood his mistake. He apologised to the raccoon and the squirrel, and then decided that from then on he would help selflessly.

And so he began helping everyone he met — chopping wood for the old deer, rebuilding an anthill, doing shopping, and watching over children. He forgot about the candies, and he felt happier than ever.

One day, when he met his friends again under the magic tree, something wonderful happened — hundreds of candies fell from the branches right into his paws.

— They're for you, Rudy! — called Kolczuś.

— But I don't want to eat them all by myself! — answered the fox. — I'll share them with those I helped, and the rest we'll eat together in our treehouse!

And so Rudy understood that the greatest joy comes not from taking, but from giving. From that day on, his good heart was known throughout the forest. And the magic tree continued to reward those who helped others out of true love.

**THE END**
